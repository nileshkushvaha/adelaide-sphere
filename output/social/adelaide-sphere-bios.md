## Facebook About / LinkedIn About

Adelaide Sphere is your guide to local Adelaide, connecting people with local businesses, places and stories.

Business listings give your business a 12-month presence in our Adelaide directory. Listings are checked and published by our editors, with contact details, opening hours, photos and customer reviews. Customers can find businesses by category, local area and search.

Have a story to share? Submit a guest article for our editors to review and publish. Published articles are clearly labelled as guest posts and listed on our blog, in search and in our RSS feed.

Explore Adelaide, list your business or submit a guest post at https://adelaidesphere.com.
